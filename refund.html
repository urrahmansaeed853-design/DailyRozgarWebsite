RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Clear rules for cancellations, refunds and payment disputes.

This policy explains how Daily Rozgar may handle eligible cancellations, refunds and payment-related disputes for platform services.

یہ پالیسی cancellation، refund اور payment dispute کے بنیادی اصول واضح کرتی ہے۔

Job cancellation

An employer or worker may request cancellation according to the applicable job status and platform rules. A cancellation request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

Eligible refunds

Where a refund is applicable, eligibility may depend on the payment status, job status, cancellation reason, work completion status and available evidence.

Completed work

Once work has been completed and confirmed, a refund is not automatically available. Genuine disputes may still be reviewed through the official support process.

Payment disputes

Users should report payment disputes through the official Daily Rozgar contact channel with the relevant job and transaction details.

Refund processing

If a refund is approved, processing will follow the applicable payment method and payment-provider procedures. Processing time may vary by payment provider.

Fraud or misuse

Refund requests involving suspected fraud, false information, unauthorized transactions or platform misuse may be investigated before any decision is made.

How to request help

Contact Daily Rozgar through the official contact channel and provide the job, payment or transaction information needed to review the issue.
