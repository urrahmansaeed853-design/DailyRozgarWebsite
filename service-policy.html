SERVICE POLICY

How Daily Rozgar services are provided and completed.

Daily Rozgar is a local job and service marketplace connecting employers with workers for daily and short-term work.

Daily Rozgar physical products ship نہیں کرتا؛ platform پر local services اور job opportunities provide کی جاتی ہیں۔

Service categories

Services may include electrical work, plumbing, carpentry, painting, masonry, general labor, home maintenance and loading/unloading helper services.

Job posting

Employers provide job details such as the required service, location, duration and agreed payment information.

Worker selection

Workers may apply for available jobs. The employer selects a worker according to the requirements of the posted job.

Service delivery

The selected worker performs the agreed work at the job location according to the job details and applicable platform rules.

Completion

A service is considered completed when the agreed work has been performed and the applicable completion process has been recorded.

Physical shipping

Daily Rozgar does not sell or ship physical products through this platform. Its primary offering is the connection of employers and workers for local services and short-term jobs.

Cancellations

Job cancellations are handled according to the job status, applicable platform rules and the Return & Refund Policy.

Service disputes

Users can contact Daily Rozgar when a service dispute, non-completion issue or payment concern requires platform support.

Need help with a service?

Contact Daily Rozgar through the official support channel for service-related questions or disputes.
