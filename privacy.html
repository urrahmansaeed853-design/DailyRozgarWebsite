Your information. Handled responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information Daily Rozgar may collect, how that information may be used, and the choices available to users of the platform.

WHAT WE MAY COLLECT

Information needed to operate the platform.

Daily Rozgar may collect information that helps users create accounts, find work, post jobs, communicate about work, and keep appropriate records of activity.

01

Account Information

This may include your name, phone number, account role, profile information and other details you provide when creating or updating your account.

اکاؤنٹ بنانے اور استعمال کرنے کے لیے نام، فون نمبر، کردار اور پروفائل کی معلومات استعمال کی جا سکتی ہیں۔

02

Job & Work Information

Job titles, descriptions, locations, applications, selected-worker information, work status and related activity may be recorded to operate the job workflow.

کام کی تفصیل، مقام، درخواستیں، منتخب کارکن اور کام کی صورتحال کا ریکارڈ پلیٹ فارم کے workflow کے لیے رکھا جا سکتا ہے۔

03

Location Information

Where the feature is enabled and permission is provided, location information may be used for relevant job or work-related features.

جہاں صارف اجازت دے اور feature فعال ہو، وہاں متعلقہ کام کے لیے location information استعمال کی جا سکتی ہے۔

04

Payment & Activity Records

Where payment or wallet features are available, relevant transaction, withdrawal, payment-status and activity records may be maintained.

جہاں payment یا wallet features دستیاب ہوں، وہاں متعلقہ payment، withdrawal اور activity records رکھے جا سکتے ہیں۔

HOW INFORMATION MAY BE USED

Information supports the Daily Rozgar workflow.

Operate accounts

Create, maintain and authenticate user accounts.

Connect workers and employers

Support job posting, applications, selection and work tracking.

Maintain records

Keep appropriate records of jobs, work activity, notifications and payments where applicable.

Improve reliability and security

Help identify technical problems, suspicious activity and service issues.

SHARING & DISCLOSURE

Information is handled for legitimate platform purposes.

Daily Rozgar does not describe user information as something that is freely available to the public. Information may be shared or disclosed when necessary to provide a requested feature, operate the service, comply with applicable law, protect users or investigate misuse.

Important: Information shown to another user may depend on the feature being used. For example, job-related information may be visible to users participating in the relevant job workflow.
YOUR RESPONSIBILITY

Keep your account information secure.

Protect Login Details

Do not share passwords, verification codes or other private account credentials with another person.

Use Official Channels

Be careful with links, messages or payment requests that claim to represent Daily Rozgar but come from unverified sources.

Keep Information Accurate

Update your profile information when necessary so that the platform can maintain useful and accurate records.

DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

Records may be retained as needed.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, meet legal requirements or support legitimate business purposes.

Appropriate technical and organizational measures may be used to help protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own account credentials and devices.

POLICY UPDATES

This policy may change as Daily Rozgar develops.

As new features, payment methods, security controls or legal requirements are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated. Important changes will be reflected on this page.
